# Standalone SaaS (Fork 1) chosen over an Atlas Freelance module or a PH Business Network partnership

## Context
[strategic-forks.md](../business/strategic-forks.md) laid out three shapes:
build standalone, ship as a module inside Atlas Freelance, or pursue a
white-label/partnership pitch to PH Business Network (JR Quilantang's
existing 160K-member audience). Needed to pick one before any product
work starts.

## Decision
Fork 1 — build as its own standalone product/brand, not folded into
Atlas Freelance and not structured around a partnership pitch.

## Why
Per [[user_solopreneur_profile]], Oliver is a solo, bootstrapped founder
— Fork 1 is the cheapest to start (no partnership negotiation overhead,
no dependency on someone else's goodwill/timeline) and avoids diluting
Atlas Freelance's "built for freelancers" positioning with an audience
(food sellers, resellers) that isn't freelancers at all. Fork 3 (PH
Business Network partnership) stays a background option to test
opportunistically, not a blocking dependency.

## Consequences
- Distribution has to be earned from zero — no existing audience to
  lean on, unlike Fork 3.
- Free to shape the product/ICP without needing to fit inside Atlas
  Freelance's existing service-freelancer positioning.
- Doesn't preclude a partnership conversation later, once there's a
  working product to show instead of just an idea.
