The problem

Most small businesses price by feel, not by math.

A cost you never separated from your price is a cost you never really knew. Ingredients, labor, packaging, overhead — most owners bundle it all into a round number that "feels right," then wonder where the profit went.

The pain is proven, not assumed: one Google Sheets template solving exactly this problem has sold to 2,000+ food business owners on a single Philippine marketplace. People already know they need this. They just haven't had a real app for it.

A spreadsheet is a snapshot, not a system. Raise the price of eggs, and you have to hunt down every recipe that uses them by hand. Run a food stall and take event gigs, and you need two separate sheets that never talk to each other.
