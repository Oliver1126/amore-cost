Three AI-image-generator prompts for a Calcost logo, built around the
visual direction already noted in [options.md](options.md)'s "Visual/
voice direction" section: peso sign (₱) as a recurring anchor, and a
warmer/grounded feel over a slick VC-pitch-deck aesthetic, since that's
closer to what this ICP already trusts.

## 1. Icon mark — calculator + peso fusion

```
Design a minimalist, modern logo icon for "Calcost", a Philippine SaaS
app that helps small business owners calculate product costs and set
profitable prices. Combine a simplified calculator button grid (2x2 or
3x3 keys) with a Philippine peso sign (₱) integrated cleanly into the
negative space or as one of the keys. Flat vector style, single
rounded-square badge shape suitable as an app icon, two-color palette
of deep teal (#0F766E) and warm amber/gold (#F59E0B) on a white or
transparent background. Clean geometric lines, no gradients, no
photorealism, no text.
```

## 2. Wordmark with a cost-to-profit accent

```
Design a modern SaaS wordmark logo reading "calcost" (all lowercase),
in a friendly rounded geometric sans-serif (similar to Poppins or
Manrope, medium-bold weight). Accent the letter "C" at the start with a
small upward arrow or ascending bar-chart motif to hint at margin
growth, in warm amber/gold against a deep teal or forest-green
wordmark. Flat vector, clean and approachable, not corporate-cold —
built for a finance app used by Filipino small-business owners. White
background, no shadows, no 3D effects.
```

## 3. Abstract badge — peso-into-arrow symbol

```
Design an abstract, minimal app-icon logo mark for "Calcost." Concept:
a single Philippine peso sign (₱) whose top stroke subtly becomes an
upward arrow or checkmark, symbolizing turning cost into profit.
Enclosed in a soft rounded-square or circular badge. Flat vector
illustration, two-tone palette (deep teal #0F766E base, warm gold
#F5A623 accent), no gradients, no text, no photorealistic elements —
must read clearly at small sizes like a mobile app icon.
```
