# Launch checklist — Phase 1 validation

The step-by-step version of [../business/go-to-market.md](../business/go-to-market.md)'s
Phase 1 plan and [../roadmap/roadmap.md](../roadmap/roadmap.md)'s Phase
1 section. Nothing here has been done yet — this is the plan, not a
log. Check items off as they actually happen; don't mark something done
because it's ready to do.

## Before posting anything

- [x] **Deploy the app** so there's a real link to share — done
      2026-08-21: **https://amore-cost.vercel.app** (decision 0031).
      Nothing below can start without this — it no longer blocks.
- [ ] **Decide go/no-go criteria in writing, before posting** — per
      `roadmap.md`'s explicit instruction: decide the bar *before*
      responses come in, so it can't quietly move to match whatever
      shows up. Suggested starting point (edit before committing):
      how many people say "I'd use this" out of how many who see it?
- [ ] **Resolve the two open questions this decides**, so the ask
      itself is sharp:
  - Is monthly willingness-to-pay real here, or does this market only
    accept one-time pricing? ([../business/concept.md](../business/concept.md))
  - Whether real accounts are worth building at all before this
    signal comes in ([../product/scope-v1.md](../product/scope-v1.md))

## The ask itself

- [ ] Lead with **"would this replace your spreadsheet"** — not "would
      you pay." Soft validation first, matching Atlas Freelance's own
      Phase 1 move.
- [ ] Use [../branding/pitch-one-pager.html](../branding/pitch-one-pager.html)
      (or a screenshot of it) *or* the live app itself — now that the
      app exists and is deployed, pointing at the real thing is
      stronger than the static pitch alone.
- [ ] Position as **"the upgrade from the sheet you already bought,"**
      not a frontal attack on the community where the competing
      template is sold (per `business/branding/pitch.md`'s note).

## Where to post, in order

1. **PH freelancer/small-business Facebook groups, r/phcareers,
   OnlineJobs.ph-adjacent communities** — same rooms Atlas Freelance
   validated in, likely real audience overlap.
2. **PH Business Network** — same audience, 160K members; mind the
   positioning care-point already flagged in `business/concept.md`'s
   open questions before posting here specifically.
3. **TikTok/short-form, creator-adjacent** — not paid ads. JR
   Quilantang and Recurra already prove this ICP responds to
   creator-led financial content; this is about finding a similar
   voice or angle, not necessarily posting under Amore Cost's own
   account first.

## After the first responses land

- [ ] **Do things that don't scale.** DM or call the first 20–50 people
      who react — walk them through it personally. Don't wait for a
      self-serve funnel.
- [ ] **Collect real testimonials and 1–2 named case studies** (e.g.
      "a home baker in Quezon City" style) — these are the actual
      currency for the next round of community posts, not ad spend.
- [ ] **Track against the Phase 1 signal**, not vanity metrics: real
      "I'd use this" reactions, not likes/shares/views. See
      `go-to-market.md`'s "what success looks like, by phase."

## What this checklist is explicitly not

- Not a paid-ads plan — per `go-to-market.md`, no ad spend before
  organic community signal confirms it's worth it.
- Not a referral program — that's a Phase 5 move, once there's a real
  paid base to refer from, not before.
- Not the PH Business Network *partnership* conversation (Fork 3) —
  that's a background lever for later, once there's a validated
  product to show, not a Phase 1 dependency.
